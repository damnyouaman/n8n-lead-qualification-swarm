Inbound Lead Qualification & Routing Swarm

n8n workflow · 14 nodes · ID 5EjpPLqvYf9l75W7 · Endpoint POST /webhook/inbound-lead
STAGE 1  —  ENRICH & SCORE
Lead Webhook
n8n-nodes-base.webhook
Catches the website form POST. Reads name, email and company from $json.body. Replies 200 immediately so the form never waits on the AI.
→
Enrichment Agent
langchain.agent
Researches the company on the web and writes a 3-paragraph brief: what they do, firmographics, recent buying signals. Instructed to write "unknown" rather than invent figures.
MODELOpenAI — Enrichment · gpt-5-mini · drives the research loop
TOOLSearch Google for company info · SerpAPI · the agent's only source of facts
→
Score Lead
langchain.chainLlm
Grades the brief against the ICP rubric (size 30 / industry 25 / signals 25 / email 10 / geography 10). Disqualifiers cap the score at 20. Unknown fields score the midpoint, never zero.
MODELOpenAI — Scoring · gpt-5 · shared with the parser and the email writer
PARSERScore Output Parser · forces JSON {score, reasoning, data_confidence}; auto-fix retries malformed output
STAGE 2  —  ROUTE & ACT
Normalize Lead + Score
n8n-nodes-base.set
Flattens everything into one clean item: name, email, company, brief, score, reasoning, confidence. Single place to fix if the score shape shifts.
→
Hot Lead?
n8n-nodes-base.if
The traffic director. Numeric test: score ≥ 80. True output feeds the sales path, false output feeds nurture.
→
HOT
≥ 80
HubSpot — Assign to AE
hubspot · contact: upsert
Creates or updates the contact and assigns the Account Executive via contactOwner.
→
Slack — Alert Sales Team
slack · message: post
Posts to #sales-alerts with name, company, score, confidence, reasoning and the full brief.
→
Write Intro Email
langchain.chainLlm
Drafts a <120-word intro opening on one real detail from the brief. Barred from citing any figure not in the brief.
→
Gmail — Draft Intro Email
gmail · draft: create
Creates a draft only — never sends. Waits in the rep's folder for human review.
COLD
< 80
HubSpot — Add to Nurture List
hubspot · contactList: add
Drops the lead into the 30-day marketing list by email. No rep is alerted, keeping the sales calendar clear of low-propensity buyers.
Cold leads stop here by design — no Slack alert, no email draft, no AE assignment.
Trigger
AI / LLM
Logic
Action / integration
Reliability: both LLM steps and all three integrations retry twice on failure. HubSpot, Slack and Gmail use onError: continueRegularOutput, so a CRM hiccup can never swallow the Slack alert for a hot lead.  ·  Before going live: set contactOwner (AE owner ID), listId (nurture list ID), confirm the Slack channel, and fund the OpenAI API key.
